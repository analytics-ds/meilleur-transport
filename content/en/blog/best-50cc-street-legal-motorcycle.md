## What is a 50cc street legal motorcycle and who can ride it?

A **50cc street legal motorcycle** is a motorized two-wheeler **conforming to road code**: registered, equipped with lights, turn signals, speedometer, and **factory-restricted to 28-30 mph** (regulatory limit for mopeds in EU and most US states). It differs from a 50cc dirt bike which is closed-track only without plates. Street-legal use covers **3 clearly differentiated user categories** in Europe and many US states:

The **teen 14-17 years old** with a **moped permit** (AM in France, M1 endorsement in some US states, or local equivalent). This is the dominant profile: 80% of new 50cc street-legal sales in Europe. The moped permit is obtained at 14 with 8 hours of theoretical and practical training, with no final exam in most jurisdictions.

The **adult 18+ without car licence**. Rare but legal case: any adult can ride a 50cc street legal with only the moped permit. For modest urban use, it is an economical alternative to the full motorcycle licence.

The **adult with A1 or full motorcycle licence** (or US M endorsement). Any motorcycle licence holder can ride a 50cc, as a complement or occasional ride. For higher-displacement comparison, see also the [best A2 motorcycle](/en/blog/best-a2-motorcycle/) guide for restricted-licence options up to 47 hp.

## Manual or automatic transmission: which to choose?

The choice between **manual transmission** and **automatic CVT** is the decisive point for 80% of 50cc street-legal buyers.

The **5-6 speed manual transmission** (Beta RR, Aprilia RX, Sherco SE, Rieju MRT, Derbi Senda) teaches **real motorcycle riding**: clutch, shifter, RPM management. It's the ideal choice for the teen who wants to take the A1 licence at 16 and continue on bigger bikes after. Essential learning for 80% of future motorcyclists.

The **automatic CVT transmission** (scooters Yamaha BWs, Peugeot Kisbee, Honda Vision) demands **no riding skill**. Adapted to pure urban use, to the adult without progression intent, or to utility use. ## 2-stroke or 4-stroke: performance vs economy

The **2-stroke** (Beta RR, Aprilia RX, Sherco, Derbi, Rieju) offers **more torque at equivalent rpm** in 50cc displacement, characteristic engine sound, and contained weight (210-230 lbs). But demands oil-fuel mix, more noise, and more pollution. It's the performance choice for the sport-minded teen.

The **4-stroke** (Mash, Fantic, scooters) consumes less (60-95 mpg vs 35-50 mpg in 2-stroke), produces less noise, and demands less engine maintenance. But offers lower performance (top speed similar due to 28-30 mph restriction, but lower acceleration). It's the economical and practical choice for the adult.

## Derestriction: why it should be avoided

**Derestriction** consists of removing the restriction elements (intake ring, transmission spring, exhaust wall) to reach speeds of **45-55 mph** instead of the legal 28-30 mph. **This practice is strictly illegal** on public roads, and exposes to:

- **$1,700 fine** and possible vehicle confiscation
- **Immediate insurance cancellation** in case of accident
- **Liability not covered**: the rider pays in full for damages caused to others
- **Moped permit suspension** and impossibility to obtain A1 or full motorcycle licence for 1-3 years

The 28-30 mph restriction is **a real safety compromise**: a 14-year-old rider lacks experience to handle a derestricted 50cc at 50 mph without assistance, and accident statistics confirm this massively. To go faster, wait until 16 to take the A1 licence and acquire a 125cc legally.

## FAQ best 50cc street legal motorcycle

### Which is the best 50cc street legal motorcycle for a 14-year-old?

The **Beta RR 50 Racing** ($4,599) stays the reference for a teen wanting to learn riding with a 6-speed manual and Italian quality. For a more contained budget, the **Rieju MRT 50 SM Pro** ($3,590) or **Derbi Senda DRD 50** ($3,990) offer excellent price-performance compromise. The moped permit is mandatory from age 14.

### Which 50cc street legal for an adult without car licence?

For an **adult without car licence**, target an **automatic 4-stroke 50cc scooter**: Yamaha BWs 50 ($2,990), Peugeot Kisbee 50 ($2,290), or Honda Vision 50 ($3,200). The automatic transmission avoids learning the manual, and the **4-stroke** consumes 30% less than a 2-stroke. Moped permit sufficient.

### 2-stroke or 4-stroke in street-legal 50cc?

The **2-stroke** (Beta, Aprilia, Sherco) offers better performance and characteristic sound, but consumes more, pollutes more, and demands oil-fuel mix. The **4-stroke** (Mash, Fantic, scooters) consumes less, runs quieter, and demands less maintenance. For a sport-passionate teen, target 2-stroke. For utility use, 4-stroke.

### Is a full-face helmet required for 50cc street-legal riding?

The **DOT-approved or ECE 22-06 helmet** is mandatory in most US states and EU for any motorized two-wheeler. The **full-face helmet** is more protective but warmer in summer; modular or open-face helmets are allowed but offer less protection. For regular road use, target a full-face DOT or ECE between $110 and $290.
