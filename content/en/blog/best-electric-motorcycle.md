The **best electric motorcycle** in 2026 stands as a credible alternative to gasoline bikes for urban commuting and dirt riding, less so for long-distance touring. The best electric motorcycle picks combine real range, fast charging, fair pricing and proven reliability. Expect **$3,500 to $30,000 USD** depending on range and power, charge times from 1 to 8 hours, and a running cost cut by 4 versus an equivalent [best A2 motorcycle](/en/blog/best-a2-motorcycle/) gasoline bike. This guide reviews **10 street-legal and off-road models**, the real range vs WMTC range gap, the 5-year ownership cost, and the licensing rules across the US, EU and UK.

## Criteria for choosing an electric motorcycle

**Real range** is the number one criterion on an electric motorcycle. The useful figure is the highway range at 65-75 mph, which represents **50 to 60% of advertised WMTC range**. A Zero SR/F ZF15.6 lists 161 miles WMTC and delivers 80-100 miles in real-world highway riding.

**Charge time** depends on the onboard charger and outlet type. On a 110V household outlet, count 6 to 10 hours for 0-100%. On CCS Combo DC fast chargers (Zero Charge Tank, Energica DC), 40 minutes to 2 hours.

**Power** determines license requirements and class (US-state dependent, A1/A2/A in Europe). Below 11 kW = beginner permits OK; 11-35 kW = intermediate; above 35 kW = full motorcycle endorsement. Most manufacturers ship with software-selectable power levels.

**Use case** drives the segment: daily commute (Super Soco, Silence S01), versatile street (Zero S/SR/F, LiveWire One), off-road dirt (Sur-Ron, Talaria, Stark Varg), hyper-sport track (Energica Ego+).

**Federal and state incentives** in the US can shave $1,500-3,000 off the purchase price on qualifying street-legal electric motorcycles, with stacking state-level rebates in CA, OR, NY and CO.

## Top best electric motorcycle models by segment

### Premium street-legal electric motorcycles

![Premium electric motorcycle, modern design](/images/meilleur-moto-electrique/premium.webp)

#### Zero SR/F ZF15.6

The **Zero SR/F** is the reference street-legal electric motorcycle naked roadster. **110 hp peak**, 0-60 mph in 3.0 seconds, 17.3 kWh battery, **161 miles** WMTC range. Optional CCS Combo charging (6 kW) for 1 hour at fast chargers. Aluminum frame, adjustable Showa fork, Bosch ABS Cornering. Price **$24,995 USD** before incentives.

#### LiveWire One

The **LiveWire One** (formerly Harley-Davidson LiveWire, now standalone brand) delivers **105 hp**, 15.5 kWh battery, **146 miles** WMTC range, and 1-hour CCS DC charge. Drag-style riding position, premium finish, Showa Big Piston fork, Brembo brakes. Price **$24,990 USD**. The premium American option, alternative to Zero and Energica.

#### Energica Ego+

The **Energica Ego+** is the Italian sport electric motorcycle. **171 hp**, 0-60 mph in 2.6 seconds, top speed 150 mph, 21.5 kWh battery. **DC fast charge 24 kW**: 0-80% in 40 minutes. Trellis frame, Brembo brakes, Öhlins suspension. Price **$28,800 USD**. Premium choice for hyper-sport feel in silence.

### Off-road electric motorcycles

![Off-road electric motorcycle Sur-Ron style](/images/meilleur-moto-electrique/offroad.webp)

#### Sur-Ron Light Bee X

The **Sur-Ron Light Bee X** is the global reference for lightweight off-road electric motorcycle riding. **8 hp peak**, 110 lbs (50 kg), removable 60V 32Ah battery (1.9 kWh), 50 miles range. Sold off-road at **$4,990 USD** or as L1e street-legal (30 mph max) at $5,690.

#### Talaria Sting MX4

Direct competitor to the Sur-Ron: 11 hp peak, 132 lbs (60 kg), 60V 45Ah battery, 56 miles range. Inverted Fastace forks, Tektro 220mm disc brakes. Price **$4,690 USD** off-road. More powerful than the Sur-Ron stock, more expensive on parts.

#### Stark Varg

The **Stark Varg** is the Swedish premium dirt bike option. **80 hp** peak (selectable in software), 245 lbs (111 kg), 6 kWh battery, 1.5 hours of intense MX riding before recharge. Priced at **$13,900 USD**, it competes head-on with high-end gasoline 250-450cc dirt bikes on closed circuits, with the silence and instant torque advantages of electric. The phone-as-dashboard interface is unusual but functional.

### Accessible street electric motorcycles

#### Super Soco TS Street Hunter

Entry-level street-legal electric motorcycle. **4 hp**, 50cc-equivalent class, accessible on regular driver's license in many US states. 62 miles range, removable battery. Price **$4,590 USD**. Limited freeway capability: strictly an urban commuter. For lighter two-wheel options, the [best 125cc motorcycle](/en/blog/best-125cc-motorcycle/) gasoline alternatives stay competitive.

#### Silence S01

Spanish electric motorcycle equivalent to a 125cc. 10 hp, WMTC range 83 miles, removable 5.6 kWh battery on wheels (chargeable indoors). Top speed 60 mph, accessible to learner riders in most US states. Price **$7,290 USD**. Removable battery is the key differentiator: no need for an outdoor outlet or apartment garage charging.

## Electric motorcycle vs gasoline bike: which to pick?

For **urban commutes under 30 miles per day**, the electric motorcycle wins: silence, no clutch, no transmission, overnight charging, ZEV access, running cost cut by 4. Minor downside: 30-50 lbs heavier than a gasoline equivalent.

For **road trips above 125 miles**, the gasoline bike keeps the lead: no charge planning, 220-280 mile tank range, 3-minute refueling. That's the natural ground for a roadster like the [Yamaha MT-07 or MT-09](/en/blog/yamaha-mt07-vs-mt09/).

For **off-road dirt riding**, the rise of Sur-Ron, Talaria and Stark Varg has shifted the trend: silent, instant torque, no clutch to manage, less impact on trails and wildlife. Electric enduro is gaining ground over 2-stroke and 4-stroke gasoline bikes in amateur competition.

## 5-year ownership cost

Purchase price varies by 7x across the segment. **Federal and state incentives** can subtract $1,500-3,000 USD on qualifying street-legal models above 3 kW.

**Energy** runs $1.00-$1.80 per 100 miles on home charging, versus $5-7 per 100 miles for an equivalent gasoline bike. For 5,000 miles a year: $50-90 in energy versus $250-350 in gasoline.

**Maintenance** on an electric motorcycle is minimal: no oil changes, no air filters, no spark plugs. Brake pads every 18,500 miles, tires every 7,500-11,000 miles. Over 5 years, $600-1,000 total.

**Premium battery packs** (Zero, Energica, LiveWire) carry 5-year or 80% capacity warranties. Replacement past warranty runs $5,000-8,000 USD before labor. On Sur-Ron and Talaria, battery packs replace at $700-1,200 USD after 1,000-1,500 full cycles.

The **break-even** electric vs gasoline lands around year 4 for daily commuters (5,000+ miles per year).

## FAQ best electric motorcycle

### Which electric motorcycle is best for beginners in 2026?

The **Super Soco TS Street Hunter** ($4,590) remains the most accessible to start with on a standard driver's license. For a step up, the **Silence S01** ($7,290) with its removable battery and 83-mile range is more versatile for daily use.

### What is the real highway range of an electric motorcycle?

Real range at 65-75 mph represents **50 to 60% of advertised WMTC range**. A Zero SR/F ZF15.6 (161 miles WMTC) delivers 80-100 miles in real highway use. For longer rides, the Zero with Charge Tank or the Energica with 24 kW DC charging stay the most credible options.

### How much does an electric motorcycle battery cost to replace?

On premium models, **$5,000-8,000 USD** before labor. Manufacturer warranty is typically 5 years or 80% residual capacity. On Sur-Ron and Talaria, replacement runs $700-1,200 USD after 1,000-1,500 full cycles, or 6-8 years of normal use.
