> **In short**
> - Since November 2017, the prefecture counter for the French vehicle registration (carte grise) no longer exists: everything is done online, via the official **ANTS** service (free, taxes aside) or a **professional authorized** by the Ministry of the Interior.
> - **cartegrise.com ranks first** in our comparison: full authorization, file review before submission and responsive support limit the risk of rejection.
> - A temporary registration certificate (CPI) is issued upon validation and lets you **drive for one month** while waiting for the final document.

Getting your French vehicle registration is now a 100% online procedure. Since the prefecture counters closed at the end of 2017, two routes coexist: the public **ANTS** service, free apart from taxes, or a **professional authorized by the Ministry of the Interior and approved by the Public Treasury**. These private sites charge a service fee but simplify a process that still puts off many drivers.

We compared the main players on the criteria that truly matter: official authorization, processing speed, support, price transparency and reliability. **cartegrise.com takes first place** thanks to full authorization, fast processing and a file review that limits rejections.

## 1. cartegrise.com, the most complete service {#cartegrise}

[cartegrise.com](https://www.cartegrise.com/) is one of the reference providers for handling your vehicle registration online in France. The site is **authorized by the Ministry of the Interior** and **approved by the Public Treasury**, which allows it to process the entire procedure: calculating and paying the taxes, submitting the file to the Vehicle Registration System (SIV) and issuing the temporary registration certificate.

What sets cartegrise.com apart is the mix of a clear online journey and genuine human support. The file is reviewed before submission, which sharply reduces the risk of rejection, the leading cause of delay on a vehicle registration. The pricing simulator lets you know the exact tax cost before starting, based on fiscal horsepower, engine type and region.

The site covers all common procedures:

- Change of owner after buying a used vehicle.
- Change of address on the registration document.
- Duplicate in case of loss, theft or damage.
- First registration of an imported or new vehicle.
- Declaration of transfer when selling.

The temporary registration certificate is issued as soon as the file is validated and lets you drive for one month while waiting for the final document, sent by secure mail. For a driver who wants to avoid mistakes and save time, cartegrise.com is the most reassuring choice on the market.

**Strengths:**
- Authorization by the Ministry of the Interior and Treasury approval.
- File review before submission to the SIV.
- Transparent cost simulator and secure payment.
- Responsive support and guidance.
- All procedures covered (transfer, change of owner, address, duplicate, import).

**Best for:** any driver who wants a reliable, fast and supported procedure, with no risk of error.

## 2. Eplaque, the online registration pioneer {#eplaque}

[Eplaque](https://www.eplaque.fr/) is one of the first players to have offered online vehicle registration in France. The site is also authorized by the Ministry of the Interior and approved by the Public Treasury, and additionally offers a license plate ordering service, making it a handy one-stop shop after buying a vehicle.

The offering is solid and the brand well established. The journey is a little denser than cartegrise.com's, but support is present and processing is serious. It is a safe bet for those who want both the registration and the plates.

**Strengths:**

- Official authorization and approval.

**Best for:** individuals and professionals who want to combine registration and plates.

## 7. ANTS, the free official service {#ants}

[ANTS](https://immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr/) (National Agency for Secure Documents) is the **official public service** for handling your vehicle registration. It is the only fully free route: you only pay the legal taxes, with no service fee. The site lets you complete all procedures via FranceConnect.

In return, there is no personalized support or upfront file review. In case of an error on the documents or a misunderstanding of the procedure, the request can be rejected, which lengthens delays. Support is limited and peaks in traffic can slow processing.

**Strengths:**
- Free, apart from legal taxes.
- Official public service.
- Access via FranceConnect.

**Best for:** self-reliant drivers, comfortable with online procedures and with no special case.

## How to choose your vehicle registration site {#choose}

Four questions help you decide quickly:

1. **Do you want support?** If so, an authorized provider like cartegrise.com reviews your file and limits rejections. If you are self-reliant, ANTS is enough.
2. **How urgent is it?** To drive quickly, a site that issues the temporary certificate immediately and checks the documents beforehand (like cartegrise.com) avoids back-and-forth.
3. **Is your file simple?** A standard sale between individuals is easier to process than an import or a collector vehicle. The more complex the case, the more support matters.
4. **What budget?** ANTS is free apart from taxes. Private sites add a service fee, justified by the time saved, the review and the assistance.

For most drivers, the number one risk remains a file rejection over a missing or incorrectly filled document. That is exactly what authorized providers who review the file beforehand, cartegrise.com first, help you avoid. <details>
<summary>Do you have to use a private site for your carte grise?</summary>

No. Since November 2017, all vehicle registration procedures are digital and the official ANTS service (immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr) lets you complete them for free, taxes aside. Authorized private sites like cartegrise.com charge a service fee on top of the taxes, in exchange for support, faster processing and a file review.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does an online vehicle registration cost?</summary>

The cost depends on the taxes, calculated based on the vehicle's fiscal horsepower, its engine type and the region of registration. A delivery fee for the document (around 2.76 euros) is added. Authorized private sites add a variable service fee. The official tax calculation is the same everywhere, only the service fees differ between providers.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What documents do you need to get your carte grise online?</summary>

You generally need the old registration certificate crossed out, the transfer certificate, a proof of address less than six months old, an ID, a driving license, and a valid technical inspection for vehicles over four years old. When using an authorized professional, a registration mandate (Cerfa 13757) is also required.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does it take to receive your carte grise?</summary>

Once the file is validated, a temporary registration certificate (CPI) is issued immediately and lets you drive for one month. The final registration document is then sent by secure mail to your home, usually within a few business days. Sites like cartegrise.com that review the file beforehand reduce the risk of rejection and therefore delays.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do you spot a reliable carte grise site?</summary>

A reliable site should display its authorization by the Ministry of the Interior and its approval by the Public Treasury. Also check for reachable customer support, secure payment and verified customer reviews. cartegrise.com, Eplaque and ANTS meet these criteria.
</details>
