## In short

1. **AMV** ranks first for insuring a classic car in 2026: the broker accepts vehicles from **15 years old** (versus 25 to 30 years at most insurers), pays out at **collection value** rather than book value, and covers both classic cars and classic motorcycles. On accessibility and flexibility of a dedicated policy, it is the most open offer in the comparison.
2. **Allianz** (Classic Car, agreed value) and **April** complete the podium with solid cover, but stricter eligibility on vehicle age. **AXA**, **Solly Azar** and **Macif** offer decent policies, more geared to occasional use.
3. Classic car insurance costs **less than standard cover** because the vehicle is driven little, often **3,000 to 6,000 km per year**. In return, the policy requires a locked garage, an experienced driver and, in most cases, another vehicle for daily trips.

A classic car is not insured like a recent saloon. Price, cover and above all the way a claim is paid rest on one principle: the **agreed value**, set jointly by the owner and the insurer. Still, you have to pick the right company, because eligibility varies widely from one policy to another. Here is the **top 6 of classic car insurers** in 2026, following on from our comparison of [motorcycle gear insurance](/en/blog/motorcycle-gear-insurance/).

## 1. AMV: the most accessible offer on the market {#amv}

<img src="/images/meilleur-assureur-voiture-de-collection/logos/amv.svg" alt="AMV logo" title="AMV logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

**Rating: ★★★★★ (4.8/5)**

**AMV**, a vehicle broker specialised since 1974, offers with its **AMV Légende** product a policy designed for old and classic vehicles. What puts it first is its entry threshold: the offer applies to **any vehicle over 15 years old** worth **less than 100,000 euros**, when competitors usually wait for 25 or 30 years. Compensation is based on the **collection value**, which reflects the collector character of the vehicle rather than the plain book value of an ordinary old car.

AMV's other strength is its dual expertise: the broker insures both classic cars and classic motorcycles, in the same tailor-made spirit. Quotes and subscription are handled online, with discounts based on the age and number of vehicles to insure.

### AMV advantages

- **Eligibility from 15 years**, the most open in the comparison, ideal for a youngtimer
- Payout at **collection value**, fairer than the standard book value
- Classic cars **and** motorcycles insured by the same specialist broker
- **Online** quote and subscription, sliding-scale multi-vehicle rates
- Vehicle appraisal, a safeguard in the event of a total loss

### AMV drawbacks

- Vehicle value capped at **100,000 euros**, which rules out the rarest pieces
- An **appraisal less than two years old** is required for damage cover
- As everywhere in the collection segment, use stays **leisure**, no daily trips

## How to choose your classic car insurance {#choose}

Three criteria settle the choice. First the **vehicle age**: a youngtimer owner of 15 to 25 years logically turns to AMV, which accepts it when others refuse. Then the **payout basis**: agreed value or collection value protects far better than a book value, especially after restoration. Finally the **use**: the collection policy assumes leisure driving, limited mileage and parking in a locked garage, in exchange for a price well below standard insurance.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Insuring a classic car on plain **third-party** cover: without agreed value, the payout after a claim collapses.
2. Under-declaring the value to pay less: the real protection is cut on the day of a total loss.
3. Overlooking the **conditions of use** (garage, mileage, secondary driver) that can suspend cover if breached.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

<details>
<summary>Which is the best classic car insurer?</summary>

For a flexible, accessible policy, **AMV** leads in 2026: the broker insures vehicles from **15 years** at collection value, cars and motorcycles alike, with an online quote. **Allianz** (Classic Car, agreed value) and **April** follow with solid cover but stricter age conditions. **AXA**, **Solly Azar** and **Macif** offer decent policies, more suited to occasional use. The best choice mainly depends on the vehicle's age and the payout basis you want.

</details>

<details>
<summary>From what age is a car considered a classic?</summary>

Administratively, the collection registration is granted from **30 years**. But insurers apply their own thresholds: most cover an old vehicle from 25 or 30 years, while **AMV** goes down to **15 years**. A vehicle of 15 to 25 years, called a youngtimer, can therefore be insured on a dedicated policy without waiting for the collection registration.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does classic car insurance cost less?</summary>

Because the vehicle is driven little, often **3,000 to 6,000 km per year**, for leisure use. This low mileage reduces the risk of a claim, hence a price below standard insurance. In return, the policy imposes conditions: locked garage, experienced driver and, at most insurers, owning another vehicle for daily trips.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is agreed value in classic car insurance?</summary>

The **agreed value** is the amount set jointly by the owner and the insurer, usually based on an appraisal, and paid in the event of a total loss. It protects far better than a book-value payout, which underestimates a restored old vehicle. AMV applies a similar logic with the **collection value**, which factors in the collector character of the vehicle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a classic motorcycle be insured with the same insurer?</summary>

Yes. **AMV** and **April** cover both cars and classic motorcycles, which simplifies management for a multi-vehicle collector. AMV, a two-wheeler broker specialised since 1974, applies sliding-scale rates based on the number of vehicles insured.
