1. The **A-Class price** runs from **€39,750** to **€86,100** according to L'Argus 2026 price lists, with a core range between **€42,000 and €45,600**
2. Against its direct rivals, the A-Class starts higher: **€5,150 above** an Audi A3 Sportback and **€8,850 above** a Volkswagen Golf at entry level
3. Three routes to the same car: **€39,750** cash for a new one, **€20,890** for a recent used example, **€350 per month** on a long-term lease with no deposit
4. After the purchase, two items really count: the CO2 penalty on combustion versions rated **128 to 134 g/km**, and a loss of value of around **35% over four years**

## How much a new A-Class costs, and where it sits in its segment {#segment}

The **A-Class price** starts at €39,750 in France in 2026, the list price of the A 180 136 hp in Star Edition trim. The top of the range reaches €86,100 with Mercedes-AMG versions.

> "The price of a new Mercedes-Benz A-Class ranges from €39,750 to €86,100."
> Source: L'Argus, new car price list, July 2026

That entry point places the compact Mercedes above its two best-selling rivals. The gap shows mainly at the bottom of the range.

| Model | Entry price | Top of range | Gap with the A-Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercedes A-Class | €39,750 | €86,100 | reference |
| Audi A3 Sportback | €34,600 | €55,900 | €5,150 less at entry |
| Volkswagen Golf | €30,900 | €58,900 | €8,850 less at entry |

The table flips at the top of the price list. The A3 peaks at €55,900 and the Golf at €58,900, two levels the A-Class only passes with its AMG derivatives. Across the core range, the versions actually ordered, all three models land in the same €40,000 to €48,000 bracket.

For a first move into a premium compact, the €5,150 gap with the A3 owes less to equipment than to what the badge is worth at resale time. That is the one line of the calculation where the A-Class takes the lead.

## Price moves mostly with the engine {#engines}

Five engines make up the 2026 line-up, all supplied with a dual-clutch automatic gearbox as standard.

| Engine | Power | CO2 (WLTP) | 2026 list price |
|---|---|---|---|
| A 180 petrol | 136 hp | 131 to 134 g/km | €39,750 to €45,650 |
| A 180 d diesel | 116 hp | 132 g/km | €41,300 to €46,950 |
| A 200 petrol | 163 hp | 131 to 134 g/km | €42,000 to €47,650 |
| A 200 d diesel | 150 hp | 128 to 129 g/km | €43,300 to €48,950 |
| A 250 e plug-in hybrid | 163 + 109 hp | 53 g/km | €49,300 to €54,950 |

The step from the A 180 to the A 200 costs just €2,250 at identical trim, for 27 extra horsepower. Moving to the plug-in hybrid costs far more: €7,300 above an A 200, a bill only urban drivers with a home charging point and company fleets manage to absorb.

Trim weighs almost as much as the engine. On an A 180, the price list runs from €39,750 in Star Edition to €45,650 in AMG Exclusive Design, €5,900 for the same 136 hp unit. The AMG Line step, billed at €2,300, brings cosmetic parts only.

## New, used or leased: three budgets for the same car {#formulas}

One A-Class means three very different budgets depending on the route chosen.

| Formula | Starting budget | Commitment | What remains at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| New, cash purchase | from €39,750 | none | a car down around 35% after 4 years |
| Recent used (2022 to 2024) | €20,890 to €32,890 | none | a car whose depreciation is already absorbed |
| Used 2012-2018 | from €15,000 | none | a car with higher mechanical risk |
| Long-term lease, 36 months | €350 per month, no deposit | 36 months, 45,000 km | nothing, the car goes back |
| Lease with purchase option, 36 months | €380 to €450 per month | 36 months | a buy-back option around 55% of the new price |

Recent used cars hold the best compromise. A 2022 example with 42,800 km was selling for €25,900 in July 2026, €15,400 below the list price of the same version new. The checks to run before signing are covered in our guide to [buying a used car](/en/blog/buying-used-car-guide/).

Below €15,000, the 2012-2018 generation comes within reach, but the dual clutch on the first-generation 7G-DCT gearbox costs €1,500 to €2,500 through the network. A complete service history matters more than a low odometer reading.

On a lease, the monthly payment depends on three levers: the deposit, the annual mileage and the buy-back value. A €5,000 deposit cuts around €130 per month over 36 months, and moving from 10,000 to 20,000 km a year adds €40 to €60. The full mechanism is set out in our comparison of offers for choosing a [Mercedes dealer for leasing in Paris](/en/blog/mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/).

## What an A-Class costs after the purchase {#after-purchase}

The list price says nothing about the real budget. Three items pile on from the first year.

**The CO2 penalty.** All five combustion engines are rated between 128 and 134 g/km of CO2, which exposes them to the French registration penalty. Only the A 250 e plug-in hybrid, at 53 g/km, escapes it. On a combustion A-Class, this line noticeably changes the price of driving off the forecourt.

**The registration certificate.** Its cost depends on the fiscal power of the car and the region of registration, with regional taxes that vary twofold from one region to another. Running the numbers through a [vehicle registration cost simulator](/en/blog/vehicle-registration-cost-simulator/) avoids a surprise on the final invoice.

**The loss of value.** This is the heaviest and least visible item. A 2022 A 180 d with 42,800 km was worth €25,900 in July 2026, while the same version new is billed at €41,300. The order of magnitude is around 35% over four years, bearing in mind that the 2022 list price was lower than today's.

That depreciation explains why trim levels matter at resale. An AMG Line finds a buyer faster than an entry-level trim, without recovering the €2,300 paid up front. AMG Exclusive Design, €5,650 above Progressive Line, remains the hardest line to justify financially.

## Where the price is actually negotiated {#negotiation}

Two channels offer the same A-Class on different terms.

At an authorised dealership, discounts commonly range from 4 to 8% on an in-stock car, and exceed 10% on an end-of-model-year or showroom vehicle. A trade-in is valued face to face and stacks with the discount.

Online intermediaries advertise up to 25% on some versions, with an entry price recorded at €38,119 in July 2026. The price is low but fixed, with no valued trade-in and no local workshop for servicing.

Stock depth is what moves the final figure: a distributor with several A-Class cars available immediately can play its own sites against each other, whereas an isolated dealership places a factory order and loses all leeway. In the Paris region, [Como](https://como.fr/pages/mercedes-benz-classe-a) runs 13 Mercedes dealerships for around 8,500 vehicles a year, one of the deepest networks in the area; the differences with a multi-brand group are detailed in our comparison of [Como or BYmyCAR for a Mercedes](/en/blog/como-vs-bymycar-mercedes-dealer/).

One last figure before signing: on an A 200 AMG Line invoiced at €44,300, six points of discount represent €2,658, roughly the cost of stepping up from Progressive Line to AMG Line.
