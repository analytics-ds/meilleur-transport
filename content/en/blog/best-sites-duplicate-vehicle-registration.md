## When to request a duplicate vehicle registration {#when}

The duplicate replaces a registration certificate that has been lost, stolen or damaged. It reproduces exactly the same information as the original registration document. In case of loss or theft, the law requires the request within a **30-day deadline**. A loss or theft declaration must accompany the procedure (**Cerfa n°13753*04** form). While waiting for the new document, a **provisional registration certificate (CPI)** allows you to drive legally for one month.

Since November 2017, the procedure is done exclusively online: prefecture counters no longer handle these requests. Only the **registered owner** of the vehicle can request a duplicate.

## 2. ANTS, the free official option {#ants}

[ANTS](https://immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr/) (the National Agency for Secure Documents) is the official route. Its advantage is cost: only the **regulatory tax** applies, with no service fee.

In return, the procedure is entirely **self-declared**, with no prior review or support. In case of an incomplete file or a bug, delays can stretch to several weeks, with no contact to unblock the situation. It is the option to favor for a simple file, when time is not a constraint.

**Strengths:**
- Completely free (regulatory tax only).
- Official public service.
- Access via FranceConnect.

**Best for:** self-reliant drivers, comfortable with online procedures and without a time constraint.

## How much does a duplicate vehicle registration cost {#price}

The regulatory cost is the same whatever the channel chosen. For a vehicle already registered in the current format (AA-123-AA), it amounts to **€13.76**: an **€11 management tax** and a **€2.76 delivery fee**. There is **no regional tax**, because a duplicate is not a new registration.

For a vehicle still registered in the old format (123 AA 00), the duplicate triggers a switch to the current format, which can generate additional taxes. Going through an authorized professional adds a service fee to this regulatory cost. ## How to choose your site for a duplicate vehicle registration {#how-to-choose}

The choice depends on the priority. To spend as little as possible and with no time constraint, ANTS is enough. To move fast, avoid file rejections and get support, an authorized professional like cartegrise.com is more suitable.

In all cases, a safety reflex applies: **check that the professional is genuinely authorized by a prefecture** before submitting your documents, as fake sites imitating the administration circulate.
