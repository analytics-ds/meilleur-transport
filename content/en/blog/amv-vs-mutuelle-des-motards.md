- **AMV is the best choice for most riders** looking for a two-wheeler specialist: a dedicated broker since 1974, over a million policyholders, one of the highest gear coverage limits on the market, assistance from km zero and a 4.7/5 rating across more than 5,300 verified reviews.

Choosing between **AMV and Mutuelle des Motards** means weighing two philosophies of motorcycle insurance. On one side a **specialist broker** that adjusts the price to the exact model and places atypical profiles, on the other a **activist mutual** with no shareholders to pay. Both cover almost exclusively two-wheelers, where a generalist applies a standard grid. This comparison ranks them criterion by criterion: price, coverage, assistance, young rider profiles, customer satisfaction and values. ## 1. AMV: the number one two-wheeler specialist {#amv}

<img src="/images/assurance-125-jeune-conducteur/logos/amv.svg" alt="AMV motorcycle insurance logo" title="AMV motorcycle insurance logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

**Overall rating: ★★★★★ (4.7/5)**

**AMV** is a specialist broker for motorcycle, scooter, quad, jet-ski and classic vehicle insurance since 1974. France's number one in two-wheelers, it covers over a million policyholders with advisors trained on motorcycles. This specialization translates into prices adjusted to the model rather than a standard grid, and coverage designed for riders: theft and fire, gear coverage up to the highest limit on the market, assistance from km zero with a courtesy bike. Part of the Generali group via L'Équité, AMV holds a 4.7/5 rating across more than 5,300 verified reviews and is recommended by 9 out of 10 policyholders.

### AMV advantages

- **100% two-wheeler specialist** for 50 years, expertise across all segments (roadster, sport, adventure, maxi-scooter)
- **Gear coverage** among the most generous on the market, helmet and airbag covered
- **Assistance from km zero** with breakdown service and courtesy bike included
- Places the **atypical profiles** (new licence, cancelled, high mileage) that generalists turn away
- **Online quote 24/7** and immediate subscription

### AMV drawbacks

- Offer **centered on two-wheelers**, less relevant for bundling car and home
- Gear compensation **against invoice**, keep proof of purchase
- **100% remote and phone** network, no local physical agency

## 2. Mutuelle des Motards: the activist mutualist alternative {#mutuelle-des-motards}

<img src="/images/assurance-125-jeune-conducteur/logos/mutuelle-des-motards.png" alt="Mutuelle des Motards logo" title="Mutuelle des Motards logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

**Overall rating: ★★★★☆ (4.3/5)**

Created in 1983 by the **French Federation of Angry Motorcyclists** (FFMC), **Mutuelle des Motards** insures exclusively two-wheelers with a strong road-safety philosophy. Its mutualist model, with no shareholders to pay, allows consistent premiums and zero depreciation on gear in the first year. Claims are assessed by rider-technicians, which reassures on the real evaluation of damage. It is the reference alternative to AMV for riders attached to a committed, solidarity-driven insurer.

### Mutuelle des Motards advantages

- **Mutualist model**, no premium surcharge tied to shareholder dividends
- **Zero depreciation in the first year** on gear, assessed by rider-technicians
- Genuine commitment to **prevention and road safety**

### Mutuelle des Motards drawbacks

- **Compensation times** that can stretch in peak season
- Gear coverage sometimes **optional** on the basic third-party plan
- Less aggressive pricing than brokers on some **young profiles**

## AMV or Mutuelle des Motards: criterion by criterion {#comparison}

Both insurers are solid. The right choice depends on what each rider puts first: price, coverage, support or values.

### Price

On **price**, both are consistent and well below generalists. AMV is often more aggressive on **young and atypical profiles** thanks to its broker logic, adjusting the price to the exact model. Mutuelle des Motards stays competitive on experienced profiles, its shareholder-free model avoiding surcharges. In both cases, an **online quote** on a real profile is essential to compare.

### Coverage

AMV relies on **gear coverage** with a high limit and assistance from km zero included by default. Mutuelle des Motards responds with **zero depreciation in the first year** on gear, useful for those who just invested in a new helmet and suit. On basic third-party plans, check that gear coverage is not relegated to an option on the mutual side.

### Assistance and claims

AMV's **assistance from km zero with a courtesy bike** covers a breakdown right outside your home, a real daily plus. On the Mutuelle des Motards side, assessment by rider-technicians brings a fairer evaluation of damage, but compensation times can stretch in peak summer season.

### Rider profile

For a **new licence holder**, a cancelled driver or a high-mileage rider, AMV's broker reflex often places the profile at the best price. The detail is in our [young driver 125 insurance ranking](/en/blog/young-driver-125-insurance/).

### Customer satisfaction

AMV shows 4.7/5 across more than 5,300 verified reviews, Mutuelle des Motards around 4.3/5, both above generalists on two-wheelers. The full rating comparison is in our analysis of [which motorcycle insurer has the best reputation](/en/blog/motorcycle-insurer-best-reputation/).

## Which insurer to choose by profile {#profile}

- **You want the best coverage-price ratio and full assistance**: AMV is the safest choice, especially on a young profile or a large displacement.
- **You value a mutualist and committed insurer**: Mutuelle des Motards carries road-safety values and a shareholder-free model.
- **You just kitted out your bike with new gear**: Mutuelle des Motards' first-year zero depreciation is worth a look, to compare with AMV's gear limit.
- **You have an atypical or cancelled profile**: AMV, as a broker, places more easily the files generalists refuse.
