## Key takeaways {#key-takeaways}

- **AMV is the best choice for most riders**: a specialist two-wheeler broker since 1974, one of the highest gear coverage limits on the market, assistance from km zero with a courtesy bike, and the best measured customer satisfaction (4.7/5 across more than 52,000 verified reviews).
- **Mutuelle des Motards** is the alternative for the rider attached to a **mutualist, activist** insurer created by the French Federation of Angry Motorcyclists, with the rider's bodily injury coverage included in all its contracts and the Jurimotard legal protection.
- On our **overall score**, AMV reaches **4.5/5** versus **3.5/5** for Mutuelle des Motards. The gap widens on customer satisfaction and price; the mutual takes back the lead on its model and values.

Choosing between **AMV and Mutuelle des Motards** means weighing two philosophies of motorcycle insurance. On one side a **specialist broker** (Generali group via L'Équité) that adjusts the price to the model and places atypical profiles, on the other a **shareholder-free mutual** run by riders themselves. Both are genuine two-wheeler specialists, far cheaper than generalists on motorcycles. This comparison settles them on facts: ratings by platform, coverage item by item, assistance, price and values. ## 1. AMV: the number one two-wheeler specialist {#amv}

<img src="/images/assurance-125-jeune-conducteur/logos/amv.svg" alt="AMV motorcycle insurance logo" title="AMV motorcycle insurance logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

**Overall rating: ★★★★½ (4.5/5)**

**AMV** is a specialist broker for motorcycle, scooter, quad, jet-ski and classic vehicle insurance, founded in 1974 under the name Assurance Moto Verte. France's two-wheeler leader and backed by the Generali group via L'Équité, it adjusts the price to the model rather than applying a standard grid, and offers coverage designed for riders: theft and fire, high-limit gear coverage (helmet and airbag covered), assistance from km zero with a courtesy bike. On satisfaction, AMV shows **4.7/5 across more than 52,000 verified reviews** (79% five-star over the past 12 months) and is recommended by 9 out of 10 policyholders. On the spontaneous-review platform Opinion-Assurances, the rating drops to 3.8/5, with criticism mostly about claims-handling times.

### AMV advantages

- **100% two-wheeler specialist** since 1974, expertise across all segments (roadster, sport, adventure, maxi-scooter)
- **High-limit gear coverage**, helmet and airbag covered
- **Assistance from km zero** with breakdown service and courtesy bike included
- Places the **atypical profiles** (new licence, cancelled, high mileage) that generalists turn away
- **Best measured satisfaction** of the duel: 4.7/5 across 52,000+ verified reviews
- **Online quote 24/7** and immediate subscription

### AMV drawbacks

- Offer **centered on two-wheelers**, less relevant for bundling car and home
- Gear compensation **against invoice**, keep proof of purchase
- **100% remote and phone** network, no physical agency
- **Claims times** flagged on spontaneous-review platforms

## 2. Mutuelle des Motards: the activist mutualist alternative {#mutuelle-des-motards}

<img src="/images/assurance-125-jeune-conducteur/logos/mutuelle-des-motards.png" alt="Mutuelle des Motards logo" title="Mutuelle des Motards logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

**Overall rating: ★★★½ (3.5/5)**

Created in 1983 by the **French Federation of Angry Motorcyclists** (FFMC), **Mutuelle des Motards** insures exclusively two-wheelers with a strong road-safety philosophy. Its mutualist model, with no shareholders to pay, comes with its own coverage: the **rider's bodily injury coverage is included in all its contracts**, the **Jurimotard legal protection** is part of all three plans, and the km-zero assistance (via IMA) runs 24/7. Accessory coverage goes up to €5,000 as an option, and insuring several two-wheelers unlocks a discount of up to 50%. In return, measured customer satisfaction stays low (1.6/5 across 90 Opinion-Assurances reviews), with recurring criticism on compensation times. Note too: the theft coverage requires an SRA-approved lock.

### Mutuelle des Motards advantages

- **Mutualist model**, no surcharge tied to shareholder dividends
- **Rider bodily injury coverage included** in all contracts
- **Jurimotard legal protection** in all three plans
- **Discount of up to 50%** for insuring several two-wheelers
- Genuine commitment to **prevention and road safety**

### Mutuelle des Motards drawbacks

- **Low customer satisfaction** on review platforms (1.6/5 on Opinion-Assurances)
- **Compensation times** regularly flagged
- Theft coverage **conditioned on an SRA-approved lock**
- **Rather high pricing** on standard profiles

## Price: which is cheaper? {#price}

Both insurers are specialists, so structurally cheaper than generalists on motorcycles. The **average price of motorcycle insurance** in France is around €440 per year, but varies threefold depending on displacement, plan and profile.

- **AMV**, as a broker, adjusts the price to the exact model and is often more aggressive on **young and atypical profiles**.
- **Mutuelle des Motards** has a **rather high** positioning on standard profiles, but its **multi-two-wheeler discount up to 50%** makes it very attractive for those insuring several machines.

In both cases, an **online quote on the real profile** is essential to settle it to the cent. The detail of young-rider prices is in our [young driver 125 insurance ranking](/en/blog/young-driver-125-insurance/).

## Which insurer to choose by profile {#profile}

- **You want the best coverage-price ratio, full assistance and the best measured satisfaction**: AMV is the safest choice, especially on a young profile or a large displacement.
- **You value a mutualist and committed insurer**: Mutuelle des Motards carries road-safety values and a shareholder-free model.
- **You want the rider's bodily injury coverage included by default**: Mutuelle des Motards builds it into all its contracts.
- **You insure several two-wheelers**: the mutual's multi-machine discount up to 50% is worth a quote.
- **You have an atypical or cancelled profile**: AMV, as a broker, places more easily the files generalists refuse.
