## What to check before ordering a motorcycle plate {#check}

Three points determine whether a **motorcycle license plate** is legal and deserve a check before confirming the order.

The first is the format. For a motorcycle, scooter or moped, only the 210 x 130 mm plate is certified. A smaller, older or fancy plate is no longer allowed on public roads.

The second is material certification. A compliant plate is made of certified plexiglass or aluminium, with a retro-reflective film and regulated typography. Purely decorative or off-spec plates pass neither a roadside check nor a technical inspection.

The third is the accuracy of the number. The engraved number must match the registration certificate exactly. The department shown, however, can be chosen freely, as long as the region logo and department number are consistent. An error on the number means the plate has to be re-ordered.

> "Since 1 July 2017, the 210 x 130 mm format is the only one certified for motorcycles, scooters and mopeds, and riding with a non-compliant plate is an offense punishable by a 135-euro fixed fine."
> Source : Service-Public.gouv.fr, 2026

## How much does a motorcycle license plate cost? {#price}

Online, a certified motorcycle plate costs on average between 12 and 20 euros depending on the material, plexiglass being a little more expensive than aluminium. For comparison, a plate ordered at an auto center is closer to 18 to 26 euros, with any fitting on top.

The price mainly depends on the material and options. A simple aluminium plate is the cheapest, a plexiglass plate with a personalized regional logo costs a bit more, and a raised or premium plate costs more still. For everyday, compliant use, a certified plexiglass plate is more than enough.

The gap with the auto center is explained by the physical store's overheads. Online, the trade-off is a 24 to 72-hour delivery time and self-fitting, a simple operation on a two-wheeler.

## How to fit your motorcycle plate yourself {#fitting}

Fitting a **motorcycle plate** does not require a garage. Once received, it is fixed to the original mount with a riveter and the supplied rivets, in a few minutes.

The key is to check the angle and legibility of the plate once fitted. A poorly fixed or overly tilted plate can be penalized during a check, just like a non-compliant plate.

Taking two minutes to check the engraved number before fitting avoids a nasty surprise, since an error means ordering a brand-new plate.

## How to choose your motorcycle plate site {#choose}

Three criteria are enough to decide between the best sites to order a motorcycle license plate.
