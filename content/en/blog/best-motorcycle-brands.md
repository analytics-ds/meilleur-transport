The **best motorcycle brands** in 2026 split into 4 main blocks: the **Japanese** (Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki) dominating world volume with 60% of sales, the **premium European** (BMW, Ducati, Triumph, KTM, Aprilia) positioned on build quality and performance, the **American** (Harley-Davidson, Indian) on the cruiser and custom segment, and the **emerging** (Royal Enfield, CFMoto, Zontes) gaining ground on entry-level. This guide details the **10 best motorcycle brands** by positioning and the flagship models of each.

## Criteria for choosing a motorcycle brand

**Dealer network and service** drive long-term ownership. Honda and Yamaha offer 200+ dealers in Europe, BMW Motorrad and Ducati 60-80, emerging brands less than 30. A dense network guarantees access to parts, software updates, and repair work.

**Engine reliability** measures longevity before major service. Honda and Yamaha hold **50,000-75,000 miles** without rebuild. Kawasaki and Suzuki follow at 45,000-65,000 miles. BMW and Ducati sit in the same range but maintenance costs are higher. KTM and Aprilia demand more frequent service.

**Used value retention** varies by brand. Yamaha and Honda hold **60-65% of new value** after 3 years. BMW and Ducati 55-60%. Triumph and KTM 50-55%. Emerging brands depreciate faster (40-50%).

**Range coverage** sets the ecosystem. Honda and Yamaha cover everything from 50cc to 1800cc, sport, naked, adventure, cruiser. Ducati and Aprilia focus on premium sport and naked. KTM and Husqvarna on off-road and supermoto. Royal Enfield on accessible 350-650cc neo-retro.

## Top best motorcycle brands by positioning

### Japanese motorcycle brands: reliability and volume

#### Honda

**Honda** stays the **world's largest motorcycle manufacturer** by volume with over 19 million bikes sold per year (all displacements, scooters and 50-110cc included). Flagship models in Europe: **CBR1000RR-R Fireblade SP** (hyper-sport), **Africa Twin** (touring adventure), **CB650R** (A2/A naked), **CB125F** (entry 125). Strength: proven engine reliability, dense dealer network, lowest maintenance costs in the industry.

#### Yamaha

**Yamaha** is the **world's second-largest motorcycle brand** with a particularly strong sport and naked range. Iconic models: **MT-07 and MT-09** (best-selling nakeds in Europe), **R7 and R1** (sport bikes), **Tracer 9 GT** (sport-tourer), **Ténéré 700** (adventure). Strength: sport-tourer positioning with characterful CP2 and CP3 engines, advanced electronics at reasonable prices.

#### Kawasaki

**Kawasaki** focuses on **sport performance** and characterful nakeds. Models: **Ninja ZX-10R** (hyper-sport), **Z900 and Z H2** (super-charged nakeds), **Versys 1000** (sport-tourer adventure), **W800** (neo-retro). Strength: the Ninja range dominates accessible sport, the inline-four remains their signature.

#### Suzuki

**Suzuki** offers **solid value for money**. Models: **GSX-S1000 and GSX-S1000GT** (naked and GT), **V-Strom 800DE and 1050** (adventures), **Hayabusa** (hyper-sport icon). Strength: pricing 5-10% below Honda and Yamaha equivalents, proven Japanese reliability.

### Premium European motorcycle brands

![Ducati Multistrada, showcase of premium Italian motorcycle brand](/images/meilleures-marques-de-moto/europe.webp)

#### BMW Motorrad

**BMW Motorrad** dominates the **premium adventure-tourer** segment with the **R 1300 GS** (absolute reference for European motorcycle touring). Other flagships: **S1000RR** (hyper-sport), **R nineT** (neo-retro), **F 900 GS** (accessible adventure). Strength: build quality, advanced onboard electronics, high used-market value retention.

#### Ducati

**Ducati** embodies **premium Italian sport motorcycles**. Models: **Panigale V4 S and V2** (hyper-sport), **Multistrada V4** (premium adventure), **Monster 937** (naked), **Streetfighter V4**. Strength: ultra-performance Desmodromic engines, exclusive design, brand value retention on the used market.

#### KTM

**KTM** is the **Austrian off-road and supermoto** specialist. Models: **1290 Super Duke R** (extreme sport naked), **890 Adventure R** (adventure), **Duke 125/390/890** (naked range), **EXC** (pure competition enduro). Strength: off-road competition DNA, particularly characterful LC8c twin engines.

#### Triumph

**Triumph** carries the **British neo-retro and naked identity**. Models: **Speed Triple 1200 RS** (sport naked), **Tiger 1200 and 900** (adventures), **Bonneville T120 and T100** (neo-retro), **Daytona 660** (sport bike). Strength: polished design rooted in 60s-70s heritage, quality finish, coherent range.

#### Aprilia

**Aprilia** is the **Italian performance brand** in Ducati's shadow. Models: **RSV4 Factory** (WSBK reference), **Tuono V4** (hyper-powerful naked), **Tuareg 660** (adventure), **RS 660** (A2 sport bike). Strength: APRC electronics among the most advanced on the market, pure racing DNA.

### Accessible emerging motorcycle brands

#### Royal Enfield

**Royal Enfield** is the **Indian neo-retro at unbeatable prices**. Models: **Meteor 350**, **Hunter 350**, **Classic 350**, **Himalayan 450** (adventure), **Continental GT 650** (cafe racer). Strength: pricing between $4,500 and $7,500, authentic vintage design, range fitting young licenses and tight budgets.

### American motorcycle brands

#### Harley-Davidson

**Harley-Davidson** carries the **American cruiser** identity since 1903. Models: **Sportster S** (modern cruiser), **Pan America 1250 ST** (adventure), **Road Glide** (touring), **Fat Boy** (classic cruiser). Strength: brand heritage, V-twin sound and torque, ownership culture. Weakness: high maintenance costs and weight (often 600+ lbs).

## FAQ best motorcycle brands

### Which motorcycle brand is the most reliable in 2026?

**Honda** and **Yamaha** stay the two most reliable brands long-term, with engines holding 50,000 to 75,000 miles without rebuild, low maintenance costs, and a dense dealer network. For a tighter budget with similar reliability, **Suzuki** is the pragmatic Japanese option.

### Which motorcycle brand is best for beginners?

For a first motorcycle, **Honda** (CB125F, CB650R) and **Yamaha** (MT-07, R7) offer the best mix of reliability, accessibility, and resale value. **Royal Enfield** (Meteor 350) is the budget alternative for riders wanting a neo-retro look at a tight price. The [best A2 motorcycle](/en/blog/best-a2-motorcycle/) guide details A2-specific models across all brands.

### Which motorcycle brand has the best used-market value?

**BMW Motorrad** and **Ducati** retain the best resale value in the premium segment (55-60% after 3 years). **Honda** and **Yamaha** sit just behind at 60-65% on best-selling models (MT-07, Africa Twin, CB650R, Tracer 9). To avoid in recent used: emerging brands that depreciate faster due to limited dealer support.

## Which best motorcycle brand to choose in 2026?

- **First reliable bike, mid-budget**: Honda or Yamaha. Network, reliability, resale.
- **Premium bike, exclusivity**: Ducati or BMW Motorrad. Finish and brand cachet, but costly maintenance.
- **Touring and grand tourer**: BMW (R 1300 GS), Honda (Africa Twin), Yamaha (Ténéré 700).
- **Track performance**: Aprilia (RSV4), Ducati (Panigale V4), Kawasaki (ZX-10R).
- **Off-road and adventure**: KTM, Husqvarna.
- **Accessible neo-retro**: Royal Enfield (Meteor 350, $4,500).
- **American cruiser identity**: Harley-Davidson.
- **For stepping up after a 125**: target a [best adventure motorcycle](/en/blog/best-adventure-motorcycle/) in larger displacement or a Yamaha MT-07/MT-09 naked.

The brand choice depends as much on intended use as on local dealer presence. Before purchase, verify the closest dealer, service turnaround time, and parts stock for common wear items. A well-supported bike beats an exclusive brand without local network.
