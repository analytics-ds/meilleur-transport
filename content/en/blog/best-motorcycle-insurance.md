## How to choose the best motorcycle insurance? {#how-to-choose}

The best motorcycle insurance is not the same for every rider. The right contract depends on engine size, usage, the rider's experience and the budget. Four criteria make the difference.

### Third-party or comprehensive: which plan to choose?

The **third-party plan** covers civil liability, the legal minimum. The **intermediate plan** adds theft, fire and broken glass. The **comprehensive plan** also covers damage to the two-wheeler, even in an at-fault accident. For a recent or valuable motorcycle, comprehensive remains the most protective. For an older machine, an extended third-party plan with theft and fire is often enough.

### The complementary coverage that matters

**Gear coverage**, assistance from 0 km, replacement bike, legal protection and above all **rider injury cover** change the real experience after a claim. Rider injury cover, which protects the rider's own injuries, is the most often neglected item even though it is essential on a motorcycle. The level of protection also depends on the vehicle, as detailed in the comparison of the [best A2 motorcycle](/en/blog/best-a2-motorcycle/).

### The rider profile and the bonus-malus

A **new A2 licence**, a **previously cancelled** rider or a rider with a **good no-claims bonus** do not pay the same rate. A specialist broker knows how to place the profiles that generalists refuse, then supports the move to wider coverage as the bonus improves. The **young rider surcharge** generally applies for 2 to 3 years after obtaining the licence.

### The average price and quality of service

The **average price of motorcycle insurance** is around 440 euros per year, but a 125 cc on a third-party plan costs far less than a sport bike on comprehensive for a new licence. Beyond price, claims speed, adviser availability and subscription simplicity carry a lot of weight. Verified reviews and independent labels remain the best indicators of real service quality. For city use, the choice of vehicle also weighs on the bill, as shown by the comparison of the [best 125cc motorcycle](/en/blog/best-125cc-motorcycle/).
