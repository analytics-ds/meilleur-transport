## Getting your vehicle registration without ANTS: is it legal? {#legality}

Yes, it is fully legal, and even provided for by the regulations. As Service-Public.fr (2025) recalls, vehicle registration procedures are carried out online, either on the ANTS site or through a professional authorized by the Ministry of the Interior.

The point to understand is the difference between ANTS and the SIV. ANTS is the official site that serves as the online counter, the SIV is the national database that records all registrations. You can avoid the first, never the second.

The mechanism that makes delegation possible is the **Cerfa 13757 mandate**. By signing it, you authorize the authorized professional to submit your file to the SIV for you. It is this document that turns a simple intermediary into a legally recognized provider to handle your registration.

## Why go through a professional rather than ANTS {#why-pro}

Using an authorized professional is not justified for everyone, but it answers very concrete situations many drivers face.

### Delays, blocks and lack of support on ANTS {#ants-blocks}

The main frustration is blocked files. A transfer code too short, a rejected document, a file pending with no explanation: these situations are frequent and hard to unblock without direct support.

The official call center exists, but waiting times and the lack of a dedicated contact often leave the user without a solution. For a vehicle you cannot use until the registration is done, the block quickly becomes a handicap.

### What an authorized professional brings {#pro-value}

A professional such as cartegrise.com reviews the file before submitting it, which avoids most rejections. In case of a missing document, an advisor tells you exactly what to provide, where ANTS often just returns an automatic refusal.

The professional also issues the temporary registration certificate immediately, to drive without waiting. This time saving and security explain the service fees charged, which add to the official taxes that are identical everywhere.
