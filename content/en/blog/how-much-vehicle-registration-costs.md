## What does a vehicle registration cost depend on? {#what-depends}

The amount of a **registration certificate** is not set freely by the sites that sell it. It adds up several taxes defined by law, only one of which really varies from one region to another.

> According to the French Code of taxes on goods and services (articles L421-37 to L421-40), in force in 2026, the price of the registration certificate is based on a regional tax voted by the regional council, plus fixed national taxes and, where applicable, a CO2 emissions penalty.

### The 5 taxes that make up the price {#taxes}

The total breaks down into five lines:

- **The regional tax (Y1)**: the heaviest part. It equals the fiscal horsepower rate multiplied by the vehicle's fiscal power.
- **The professional tax (Y2)**: a flat rate mainly for commercial vehicles, most often zero for a private individual.
- **The ecological penalty (Y3)**: applied to the highest CO2-emitting new vehicles, it can alone exceed the total of all other taxes.
- **The management tax (Y4)**: 11 euros, a fixed national amount.
- **The delivery fee (Y5)**: 2.76 euros to send the document to your home.

A reliable site details these five lines rather than showing an opaque total. That is the first sign of seriousness to check.

### The central role of the fiscal horsepower {#fiscal-horsepower}

The fiscal horsepower is the variable that explains most of the price gaps between regions. In 2026, its rate ranges from 43 to 60 euros depending on the region of registration.

In concrete terms, for the same 6-fiscal-horsepower vehicle, the regional tax alone ranges from about 258 euros to 360 euros depending on where you live. This is why any serious calculation starts with the region or postal code. To get the exact amount for your vehicle, the simplest way is to [simulate your vehicle registration cost](/en/blog/vehicle-registration-cost-simulator/) before any procedure.

## How much does a vehicle registration cost in different cases? {#by-case}

The final price depends on the vehicle's profile. A few examples set the orders of magnitude for 2026.

For a **small city car of 5 fiscal horsepower**, the regional tax is between 215 and 300 euros depending on the region, plus the 11 euros of management and 2.76 euros of delivery. The bill is therefore around 230 to 315 euros, without any penalty.

For a **powerful, high-emitting new vehicle**, the ecological penalty changes everything. On the highest-emitting models, it can add several thousand euros to the registration cost, far beyond the regional tax alone. A car bought in another EU country adds one step before registration, the payment of French VAT, a mechanism set out on the example of a [Geely Starray bought in Europe](/en/blog/buy-geely-starray-europe/).

For a **used vehicle over 10 years old**, the regional tax benefits from a 50 percent reduction. This often-overlooked point significantly lightens the bill on older cars.

Finally, some procedures are cheap or even free. A simple [vehicle registration address change](/en/blog/best-sites-vehicle-registration-address-change/) is free for the first three, with only the delivery of the new document charged at 2.76 euros.

Since the "base" price is set by the State, the real question is where to complete the procedure. Five players dominate the search: one public service and four online operators.

### cartegriseminute.fr {#cartegriseminute}

cartegriseminute.fr also offers a free estimate and the full online procedure. The site displays prefecture authorisation 15119 and Public Treasury approval 21599, active since 2009, with about 978,000 registration certificates issued. ### carte-grise.org {#carte-grise-org}

carte-grise.org works on the same model, with the same authorisation 15119 and approval 21599 as cartegriseminute.fr, which suggests a shared operator. Processing is stated at under 24 hours and instalment payment is possible. However, the site discloses neither its precise track record nor the number of customer reviews.

### eplaque.fr {#eplaque}

eplaque.fr highlights a fast order from the licence plate number. The site is authorised by the Ministry of the Interior (18621) and approved by the Public Treasury (26579), active since 2009 with more than one million users. It shows 4.5 out of 5 across nearly 32,900 reviews, one of the highest volumes in the comparison, offers a free price simulator and instalment payment from 100 euros.

### service-public.gouv.fr, the official free reference {#service-public}

service-public.gouv.fr is the State's source. Its simulator is official, free and up to date with the scales: it is the reference for a neutral estimate. Its limit is clear: it does not complete the procedure for you and redirects to the ANTS, where the user handles the file alone.

## How to spot a reliable vehicle registration site? {#reliable}

Since the price is identical everywhere, the site's reliability makes all the difference. Two points deserve your attention.

### The criteria that matter {#criteria}

Three criteria help you decide: genuinely free access to the estimate, available without prior payment; 2026 rates updated, with the fiscal horsepower scales voted by the regions; and authorisation, with the prefecture authorisation number and Public Treasury approval displayed on the site. These same requirements apply to any procedure, including to [do your vehicle registration online](/en/blog/best-online-vehicle-registration-sites/) after a purchase.

### The pitfalls to avoid {#pitfalls}

Conversely, three signals should raise a red flag. The fake official site, which copies the State's design to charge for a service that is actually free on the ANTS. Hidden fees, revealed only at payment while the estimate showed a lower amount. And outdated rates, which give a false estimate. If in doubt about the intermediary, it is still possible to do your [vehicle registration without going through the ANTS](/en/blog/best-sites-vehicle-registration-without-ants/) via an authorised, transparent operator.
