The procedure has been digital since 2017 and can be done for free on the public service or, for a service fee, through an authorized professional who secures and speeds up processing. The transfer certificate is not an administrative detail. It is what releases the seller's liability and unlocks the buyer's new registration. Choosing the right channel means securing a valid **transfer declaration**, submitted on time and accompanied by the all-important transfer code.

## 7. ANTS, the free official service {#ants}

[ANTS](https://immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr/demarches-en-ligne/vendre-ou-donner-votre-vehicule) (the National Agency for Secure Documents) is the **official public service** to declare a vehicle transfer. ## Vehicle transfer certificate free or paid: which to choose? {#free-or-paid}

This is the most asked question on the topic, and the one most sites avoid answering. The answer is simple: the transfer declaration is **free** on ANTS, and what professionals charge for is a support service, not the document itself.

Concretely, you never pay for the **free transfer certificate** as such. At an authorized professional, you pay for assisted data entry, file review and secure submission, plus sometimes the link to the registration procedure that follows.

For a standard sale between individuals, with no difficulty, ANTS is more than enough and costs nothing. For a situation with a risk of error (transfer to a professional, imported vehicle, joint ownership, a seller uncomfortable with digital tools), the support of a player like cartegrise.com reduces the risk of a block and saves time. The right choice therefore depends on the profile and the complexity of the file.

## Vehicle transfer certificate: what to know before declaring {#good-to-know}

Understanding how the transfer works avoids the two most frequent mistakes: forgetting the legal deadline and overlooking the transfer code.

### The Cerfa 15776 form and the transfer code {#cerfa-code}

The declaration relies on the **Cerfa 15776** form, in its **15776*02** version in force since January 2022 (which requires the SIRET rather than the SIREN for professionals). It is filled in and signed by the seller and the buyer, in two copies.

Once the declaration is recorded, the system generates a **transfer code**. This code is passed by the seller to the buyer: without it, the buyer cannot issue the new vehicle registration. It is the most common blocking point, often ignored by sellers who think they are done after signing the Cerfa.

### The 15-day deadline and required documents {#deadline-documents}

The seller must declare the transfer within a **15-day deadline** following the sale. As Service-Public.fr (2025) recalls, as long as the transfer is not recorded, the seller is still considered the owner and can receive fines linked to the vehicle.

On the document side, the declaration requires the old registration certificate crossed out with the date and time of transfer, an administrative status certificate (proof of no lien) less than fifteen days old, a valid technical inspection for vehicles over four years old, and the identity documents of the seller and the buyer. A complete file is the condition for a transfer code issued without a hitch.
