1. **AMV** is the best choice to insure a Harley-Davidson over 10 years old in 2026: the two-wheeler insurance specialist covers customs under its standard motorcycle policy, with helmet, gloves and airbag vest covered **up to 2,000 euros** from the first plan. And once the bike passes **15 years**, its **AMV Légende** offer insures it at **collection market value**, far more protective than book value for a well-kept Harley.
2. **April Moto** and **Mutuelle des Motards** complete the podium: two solid two-wheeler players, but with no equivalent of a switch to a classic-bike policy at 15 years. **Solly Azar** and **Allianz** close the ranking, useful for specific profiles.

A 2008 or 2012 Harley-Davidson is no longer a new bike in the eyes of a mainstream insurer: the book value drops, parts stay expensive, and online forms often return surprisingly high quotes for an ageing Sportster or Softail. Yet several specialists know exactly how to insure this kind of machine, sometimes for less than a recent bike. ## 1. AMV: the custom reflex, from standard policy to AMV Légende {#amv}

**Rating: ★★★★★ (4.8/5)**

**AMV**, a broker specialised in two-wheeler insurance since **1974**, insures over **1 million riders** and holds a **4.7/5** rating on Avis Vérifiés. For a Harley-Davidson over 10 years old, its motorcycle policy covers the custom like any machine, with a real plus for the well-equipped rider: helmet, gloves and airbag vest covered **up to 2,000 euros from the first plan**. You choose among **4 plans** on a single contract, with theft available from the second one, which lets you adjust cover to the bike's real value.

The real asset comes when the Harley crosses **15 years**: the **AMV Légende** offer then insures it as a classic motorcycle, compensated at **collection market value**, which reflects its character and condition rather than mere book value. No collector registration needed; the price depends on the number of vehicles, their value and the machine's age bracket. A 1997 Sportster or a 2009 Dyna each find their contract, with the same broker.

### AMV pros

- Two-wheeler specialist for 50 years, claims handled by motorcycle-minded advisers
- Rider gear covered **up to 2,000 euros** from the first plan
- **AMV Légende from 15 years**: collection market value, no collector registration required
- Online quote and subscription, decreasing rates for several vehicles
- Anti-theft engraving halves the theft deductible on the motorcycle policy

### AMV cons

- Under Légende, value capped at **100,000 euros** and leisure use only (no daily commuting)
- An **appraisal less than two years old** is required for damage cover on classics
- Instalment fees when paying monthly

## How to properly insure a Harley over 10 years old {#choose}

Three questions settle the choice. First, the **bike's exact age**: between 10 and 15 years, everything happens on a standard motorcycle policy, and the quality of the guarantees (gear, theft, assistance) separates the offers. From 15 years, switching to a classic policy such as **AMV Légende** changes the compensation method, and it is often the best deal for a well-preserved machine. Then the **real value**: a maintained second-hand Harley, with its chrome and original parts, is often worth more than its book value, hence the interest of a collection market value. Finally, **usage**: a custom ridden daily stays on a standard policy; classic and collection plans assume leisure use.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Keeping an **expensive comprehensive plan** on a bike whose value has melted: past a certain age, a third-party plan enriched with theft and fire protects almost as well for far less.
2. Ignoring the **gear guarantee**: on a Harley, helmet, jacket and airbag vest quickly exceed 1,500 euros; our guide to [motorcycle gear insurance](/en/blog/motorcycle-gear-insurance/) details each insurer's limits.
3. Forgetting to declare **accessories and fitted parts** (exhaust, saddlebags, top case): undeclared, they are not compensated after theft or a fall.

<summary>Can a Harley over 15 years old switch to classic motorcycle insurance?</summary>

Yes, and without waiting for the 30-year collector registration. At **AMV Légende**, any two-wheeler over **15 years old** can be insured as a classic motorcycle, compensated at **collection market value**. The price depends on the number of vehicles, their value and the machine's age bracket. The trade-off is leisure use: for daily commuting, the standard motorcycle policy remains the right formula.

<details>
<summary>How much does insurance cost for an old Harley-Davidson?</summary>

There is no single price: it depends on the model, engine size, address, level of cover chosen and declared claims history. One thing is constant: an old motorcycle insured under a dedicated plan generally costs less than under a standard policy, because it is ridden less. The only reliable way to know your price remains the online quote, free at specialist brokers such as AMV or April Moto.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are a custom's chrome and accessories covered?</summary>

Only if they are declared on the policy. Parts fitted after purchase (exhaust, saddlebags, top case) must appear in the declaration to be compensated after theft or damage. As for rider gear, **AMV** covers helmet, gloves and airbag vest **up to 2,000 euros** from the first plan, a strong point against competitors' lower limits.

</details>

On a machine whose value has dropped a lot, a plan with theft and fire may be enough.
