The **electric bike (e-bike)** has become one of the most considered purchases for individuals seeking clean mobility. In 2026, the offer is wide, prices vary considerably and technologies continue to evolve. This comparison ranks the best models by use and budget to help make the right choice.

## Electric bikes in 2026: a rapidly expanding market

With more than **800,000 electric bikes sold in France each year**, the market has tripled in five years. Several factors explain this growth: purchase bonuses of up to **400 euros** depending on income, the continuous expansion of urban cycle lanes, rising fuel prices and a significant improvement in the quality of entry-level models.

The market has consolidated around three main families: the **urban e-bike** (lightweight, connected, focused on daily commuting), the **e-touring bike** (versatile city-to-nature riding) and the **electric mountain bike** (e-MTB, designed for trails and steep climbs). Each use case corresponds to a specific price range and set of components.

## Essential criteria for choosing an e-bike

Before comparing models, it helps to understand the variables that genuinely differentiate two electric bikes.

### Motor: hub or mid-drive

A **mid-drive motor** (Bosch, Shimano Steps, Fazua, Brose) offers better weight distribution, more natural assistance during acceleration and superior efficiency on climbs. A **hub motor** (rear or front wheel) is mechanically simpler and generally less expensive, at the cost of a higher centre of gravity and less progressive assistance feel.

### Battery: capacity and real-world range

Capacity is measured in **watt-hours (Wh)**. On standard flat terrain with moderate assistance:

- **300-400 Wh**: 50 to 70 km range
- **500-600 Wh**: 80 to 120 km
- **750 Wh and above**: 130 to 200 km

Real-world range depends heavily on elevation gain, rider weight, tyre pressure and assistance level used. Manufacturer figures are always measured under optimal conditions.

### Brakes, suspension and weight

**Hydraulic disc brakes** are recommended from 1,500 euros upwards: they provide significantly better power and modulation than mechanical cable brakes, particularly in wet conditions. E-bike weight (14 to 28 kg depending on category) directly affects low-speed handling, portability on stairs and downhill dynamics.

## Best urban e-bike: city references

An urban electric bike must be **lightweight, compact and easy to lock**. The priority criteria: weight under 18 kg, battery integrated into the frame, hydraulic brakes and smartphone connectivity for anti-theft tracking.

**The Cowboy Cross** (€3,299) is the premium benchmark in this segment: an invisible integrated battery, GPS anti-theft alarm built in, complete iOS/Android app and ultra-responsive startup assistance. Its 70 km range is more than sufficient for daily commutes of 10 to 30 km round-trip.

**The Tenways CGO600 Pro** (€1,799) is the market surprise of 2026: a carbon frame, only 14.8 kg and 100 km range at a contained price. It targets regular commuters who prioritise lightness and reliability over advanced connected features.

**The Starway Urban II** (€2,190) represents an excellent equipment-to-price compromise: Bafang 250 W motor, removable 360 Wh battery, integrated rack and mudguards as standard, with clearance suited to cobbled city streets.

## Best e-touring bike 2026: city and nature versatility

The **electric touring bike** targets cyclists who alternate daily city commutes with nature outings at the weekend. It combines a comfortable upright riding position, semi-slick tyres suitable for gravel paths and a generous battery for longer distances.

**The Decathlon Rockrider E-ACTV 900** (€2,199) leads this segment: Fazua Ride 60 motor (60 Nm torque), 430 Wh battery, Shimano hydraulic brakes and 17.5 kg weight. Sold in stores with a dense service network across France, it also offers after-sales peace of mind that online brands cannot match.

**The Cube Kathmandu Hybrid Pro 750** (€3,299) targets long-distance riding: 750 Wh battery, Bosch Performance Line CX motor (85 Nm), full-spec rack, integrated lighting and mudguards included. The ideal choice for cyclists regularly riding more than 60 km per outing.

**The Moustache Samedi 28.2** (€3,890) is the choice for users who demand premium finish and long-distance comfort. Its comfort geometry, optional dual battery (up to 1,040 Wh) and top-tier components justify its price positioning.

## Best electric mountain bike in 2026: off-road performance

The **electric mountain bike** (e-MTB) targets trail rides, forest tracks and steep climbs. Its powerful motor, full suspension and trail-specific geometry are its main distinguishing features from the e-touring bike.

**The Cube Reaction Hybrid Pro 800** (€3,799) is the 2026 reference. Its Bosch Performance CX motor (85 Nm) handles 15-20% gradients effortlessly, its 800 Wh battery delivers 120 km range in Eco mode and its trail geometry suits both beginner and experienced riders.

**The Rockrider E-FEEL 700S** (€2,999) establishes itself as the best budget alternative: 100 mm front suspension, Shimano EP8 motor known for its smooth assistance, Shimano XT hydraulic brakes, at a price well below premium brands.

## What budget for a quality electric bike?

The e-bike market is structured around four clearly distinct price ranges:

- **Under €1,000**: basic assistance, low-capacity batteries (200-300 Wh), imprecise hub motors. Acceptable only for very occasional use on flat terrain.
- **€1,000 to €2,000**: the threshold for reasonable quality. Hydraulic brakes, 360-500 Wh battery, reliable motor. The Tenways CGO600 Pro and Rockrider E-ACTV 500 sit in this range.
- **€2,000 to €3,500**: the premium mainstream market. Latest-generation Bosch or Shimano motors, 500-750 Wh batteries, lightweight butted aluminium frames. Most references in this guide belong to this range.
- **Over €3,500**: maximum component quality, specific geometries, carbon finishes on select models. Moustache, Riese & Müller, Specialized, Trek.

The **e-bike purchase bonus** in France can reach **€400** for lower-income households. This subsidy stacks with regional incentives in some areas.
