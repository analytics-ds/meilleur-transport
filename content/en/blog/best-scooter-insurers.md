## 2. Mutuelle des Motards: the mutualist commitment {#mutuelle-motards}

<img src="/images/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter/logos/mutuelle-des-motards.png" alt="Mutuelle des Motards logo" title="Mutuelle des Motards logo" loading="lazy" style="height:96px;width:auto;max-width:320px;object-fit:contain;display:block;margin:1.5em auto 1em;">

Founded in **1983 by the French Angry Bikers Federation**, **Mutuelle des Motards** is the only French mutual fully dedicated to two-wheelers. With no shareholders, surpluses go back to members. It is a credible alternative to **insure a scooter** with a strong community spirit. For two specific profiles it is actually the best pick on the market, ahead of AMV: the **new rider**, whose surcharge tapers away in just two years, and the **urban electric scooter** in daily use, where it ranks among the most generous insurers on the discount and on battery cover.

**Typical 125 scooter price**: **190 euros per year** third-party, **375 euros** third-party plus theft, **605 euros** comprehensive.

### Mutuelle des Motards advantages

- **New-rider surcharge tapering over 2 years** only
- **Free secondary rider** by default
- **0 km roadside assistance** included, claims handled by biker technicians
- Activist commitment: defending two-wheeler users beyond the **insurance policy**

### Mutuelle des Motards drawbacks

- **Mutualist membership** required (token contribution)
- Longer claims processing in peak season
- Online customer area less modern than AMV's

## Scooter insurance vs motorcycle insurance: what changes {#scooter-vs-motorcycle}

Many riders try to **insure a scooter** the way they would a motorcycle, but the two policies follow different logics. **Motorcycle insurance** covers more powerful, more accident-prone vehicles, which pushes the price up. **Scooter insurance**, especially on a 50 or an urban 125, leans more on theft risk and daily city use.

![Maxi-scooter on the road, covered by two-wheeler insurance](/images/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter-4.webp "Maxi-scooter insurance vs motorcycle insurance")

On the **motorcycle and scooter** segment, the same players dominate: specialist **two-wheeler insurance** brokers offer a single **insurance policy** able to cover a motorcycle as well as a scooter, with the same third-party, intermediate and **comprehensive insurance** formulas. So it pays to compare one insurer's **motorcycle and scooter insurance** offer, then use a **comparison tool** to confront quotes on your real vehicle.

## The average scooter insurance price in France in 2026 {#average-price}

![Scooter parked in the city, a pricing factor for scooter insurance](/images/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter-2.webp "Average scooter insurance price in France")

Even with the **best scooter insurance**, the rate depends on the vehicle and the area. The **average scooter insurance price in France in 2026** is **430 euros per year** across all formulas. The breakdown by formula:

- **Third-party**: **160 to 280 euros per year** (average **220 euros**)
- **Third-party plus theft and fire**: **330 to 480 euros per year** (average **410 euros**)
- **Comprehensive**: **560 to 760 euros per year** (average **650 euros**)

Engine size and location weigh heavily: a scooter insured in Paris costs about **25% more** than in a rural area. Classic **motorcycle insurance** stays slightly higher than a scooter of equivalent engine size, due to higher claims frequency.

## How to find the cheapest scooter insurance {#savings}

Finding the **best scooter insurance** at the best price comes down to a few simple levers that cut the **scooter insurance price** by **20 to 40%**.

### Effective levers

- **Use a comparison tool** to put insurers head to head on the same profile
- **Choose a specialist** two-wheeler broker over a generalist
- **Park in a locked garage**: 8 to 15% off the theft cover
- **SRA-approved lock**: 5 to 10% off
- **Pay annually** to avoid monthly instalment fees

![Helmet and gloves, gear covered by good scooter insurance](/images/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter-3.webp "Rider gear covered by scooter insurance")

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Picking the cheapest without reading the exclusions**
2. **Under-declaring mileage**, which can void the policy
3. **Skipping gear cover**, when helmet and jacket are expensive
4. **Overlooking 0 km assistance**, essential for daily city use

## Which scooter insurance should you choose in 2026? {#verdict}

![Scooter dashboard, choosing the best scooter insurance](/images/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter/meilleurs-assureurs-scooter-6.webp "Choosing the best scooter insurance in 2026")

For most urban riders, the **best scooter insurance** is the one that balances a fair price with solid daily-use cover, not the cheapest line on a quote. A specialist two-wheeler broker such as **AMV** wins on this segment because it combines a low **scooter insurance** premium, 0 km roadside assistance and generous helmet and gear cover, three things generalists rarely match at the same price.

The right method stays the same whatever your scooter: define your real need (city commute, weekend rides, mileage), pick the formula that fits (third-party for an older 50 or 125, comprehensive for a new maxi-scooter), then run a **comparison tool** on your exact model. For a new rider or an urban electric scooter in daily use, Mutuelle des Motards is often the best pick of all, since its surcharge tapers faster and its electric discount is among the most generous, while an experienced licence holder can claim a car no-claims discount at April Moto.

In short, the **best scooter insurance** in 2026 is AMV for the price-to-cover ratio, with Mutuelle des Motards and April Moto as strong alternatives depending on your profile. Always read the exclusions before you take out a policy, because a low premium with a high theft excess can cost more after a claim.
