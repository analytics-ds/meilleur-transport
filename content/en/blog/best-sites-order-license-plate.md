## Ordering online or at an auto center: which to choose? {#online-vs-store}

The first decision is the purchase channel. The difference plays out on three fronts: price, turnaround and fitting autonomy.

On price, the advantage clearly goes to the online site. A plate costs 8.90 to 16 euros at a pure player, against 18 to 26 euros at an auto center. The gap is explained by the overhead and labor costs of the physical store.

On turnaround, the auto center wins when you are in a hurry: the plate is stamped and handed over immediately. Online, you must wait for delivery, generally 24 to 48 hours, sometimes 72 hours for on-demand manufacturing.

On fitting, the two models differ. At an auto center, a professional can fit it for you. Online, fitting is done yourself, but it is simple: rivets are provided and the job takes a few minutes with a drill. For a **plate replacement** without urgency, ordering online remains the cheapest choice.

## Certified plate: what the regulations say {#regulations}

This is the point most sites mention without ever explaining it. Yet understanding **certification** avoids buying a **non-certified plate** and risking a fine.

### SIV certification and TPPR number {#siv-tppr}

Since 2009, France uses the **SIV**, the vehicle registration system, which assigns a number in the AA-123-AA format valid for the vehicle's lifetime. Every plate must reproduce this number according to precise rules of font, size and color.

Certification relies on two markers. **UTAC** validates the technical compliance of the material and manufacturing. The **TPPR number** identifies the authorized manufacturer that produced the plate. An SIV-compliant plate therefore carries a reference proving its legal origin.

The stakes are not just theoretical. As Service-Public.fr (2025) recalls, a non-compliant plate is a fourth-class offence, punishable by a fixed fine of 135 euros that can be raised up to 750 euros. Buying certified is therefore not optional.

### Format, materials and authorized personalization {#format-materials}

The format of a car plate is set at 520 x 110 mm, that of a **motorcycle license plate** at 210 x 130 mm. These dimensions are regulatory and identical whatever the site chosen.

Two materials dominate. **Aluminium** is the cheapest and most common, but sensitive to corrosion over time. **Plexiglass**, more expensive, offers a better rendering of characters and better resistance to UV and impacts.

The **personalized license plate** is strictly framed. You can choose the territorial identifier, that is the **regional and department logo** displayed on the right of the plate. On the other hand, changing the font, the background color or adding fancy elements makes the plate non-certified and illegal on the public road.

## How to choose your license plate site {#how-to-choose}

Four criteria are enough to decide between the best sites to order a **car license plate**.

Material first. For the tightest budget, aluminium is enough. For durability and rendering, premium plexiglass is preferable.

Turnaround next. If you can wait two days, an online site is more advantageous. If you need the plate immediately, an auto center remains unbeatable.

The need for an associated procedure last. If you also change your **registration document** (used purchase, change of address), a player like cartegrise.com lets you handle everything at once.
