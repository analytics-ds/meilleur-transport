## What is a naked motorcycle in 2026?

A **naked motorcycle** (also called a roadster or streetfighter) is a sport bike **without fairing**, with high handlebars, upright ergonomics, and standard equipment focused on city and road use. The segment differs from a sport bike by everyday comfort and from a sport-tourer by its lack of aerodynamic protection. It is the **most polyvalent motorcycle** by definition: usable daily, capable of weekend back-road blasts, and now typically equipped with modern electronics (lean-sensitive ABS, traction control, ride modes, TFT screen).

The 2026 market structures around **4 main engine characters**. The **parallel twin** offers marked vibrations, low-end torque and accessible budget. The **triple** is the balance between torque and high revs with a distinctive sound. The **inline-4** delivers smooth power and high revs. The **V-twin or V4** brings massive torque, sport sensations, and sharp character. For a more relaxed, less sport-focused option, see also the [best 125 scooter](/en/blog/best-125-scooter/) segment in accessible urban two-wheelers.

## Character 1: Parallel twin, low-end torque and contained budget

The **parallel twin** (two cylinders inline) is the **dominant engine character in entry and mid-tier**. It offers torque from 3,000 rpm, vibrations felt at the bars but managed by modern balance shafts, and accessible purchase and maintenance costs. Ideal for the beginner rider, daily commute, and the new licence holder seeking a capable restricted bike.

### Yamaha MT-07: the absolute parallel twin reference

The **Yamaha MT-07** ($8,499) stays in 2026 the **most-sold naked motorcycle in the US and Europe**. CP2 parallel twin 689cc, **73 hp**, 406 lbs, A2/restricted-friendly with kit, compact ergonomics, progressive braking, 5-inch TFT screen. Crossplane twin with 270-degree firing order that mimics V-twin sound, very linear torque from 4,000 rpm. It is the bike **80% of new riders** choose for their first serious purchase.

### Honda CB750 Hornet: the winning return

The **Honda CB750 Hornet** ($8,499) replaced the legendary CB650R in 2024 with a new 755cc parallel twin. **92 hp**, 419 lbs, traction control, ride modes, 5-inch TFT, Showa SFF suspension. Character is sportier than the MT-07, higher in the rev range, and the revisited Hornet design appeals to brand fans. The most aggressive price-performance compromise of the 2026 segment.

### Kawasaki Z650: the polyvalent compromise

The **Kawasaki Z650** ($7,899) offers a 649cc parallel twin, **68 hp**, 412 lbs, restricted-license friendly. Comfortable position, accessible ergonomics, ABS and traction control standard, Nissin braking. Smoother character than MT-07, ideal for the rider seeking **stability and daily comfort** rather than pure performance. For a detailed MT-07 vs MT-09 comparison, see also [Yamaha MT-07 or MT-09](/en/blog/yamaha-mt07-vs-mt09/) in a direct match between the two Yamaha nakeds.

## Character 2: Triple, the torque-rev balance

![Yamaha MT-09 2026, the triple-cylinder naked reference](/images/meilleur-moto-roadster/banner.webp)

The **triple** is the most **balanced** engine character in the market. It offers low-end torque of a parallel twin AND high revs of an inline-4, with a distinctive F1-like or racing sound. More mechanically complex, it costs more but offers exceptional polyvalence between urban riding, road use, and sporty back-road runs.

### Yamaha MT-09: the accessible triple reference

The **Yamaha MT-09** ($11,099) is the **best-selling triple** in the US and Europe. CP3 890cc, **119 hp**, 425 lbs, 5-inch TFT screen, KYB adjustable suspension, Brembo brakes. Y-AMT automatic transmission optional (+$1,300), Sport/Street/Rain/Custom ride modes, traction control, lean-sensitive ABS. **Sharp but civilized** character, capable of playing in corner exits and holding pace daily.

### Triumph Street Triple 765 RS: the accessible racing triple

The **Triumph Street Triple 765 RS** ($14,295) is Triumph's most accessible triple. **130 hp** on 414 lbs, fully adjustable Öhlins NIX30 suspension, Brembo Stylema brakes, 5-inch color TFT, 6-axis IMU. Its 765cc triple is the engine of Moto2 (world championship), with a distinctive sound and addictive rev climb. Slightly sportier ergonomics than the MT-09.

### Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RS: the premium triple

The **Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RS** ($20,395) pushes the triple to the extreme. 1160cc, **180 hp**, 437 lbs, fully active Öhlins SmartEC 3 suspension, Brembo Stylema brakes, 6-axis IMU, lean-sensitive traction control, 5-inch TFT. **Brutal but precise** character, for the experienced rider who wants the triple in its most extreme form.

## Character 3: Inline-4, smoothness and high revs

The **inline-4** is the **historic** engine character of Japanese and European sport bikes. It delivers linear and smooth rev climb, minimal vibration even at 10,000 rpm, and **accessible top-end power**. More complex and more expensive, it targets the experienced rider who enjoys exploiting high revs and precision.

### BMW S 1000 R: the sport bike turned naked

The **BMW S 1000 R** ($17,495) takes the S 1000 RR (sport bike) engine in naked form. **170 hp** on 439 lbs, DDC semi-active suspension, 6.5-inch TFT, Pro ride modes, DTC traction control, M Brembo braking. Higher position than the sport bike, naked ergonomics, but the character and sound are **sport bike racing typed**. For the experienced rider who wants **pure performance** in a polyvalent format.

### Honda CB1000 Hornet SP: the Honda 4-cylinder return

The **Honda CB1000 Hornet SP** ($14,499) marks Honda's return to the inline-4 naked segment. Inline-4 1000cc, **157 hp**, 465 lbs, Showa Big Piston Fork inverted suspension, Brembo Stylema brakes, 5-inch TFT. Calmer character than European competitors, **legendary Honda reliability**, and more contained price than the S 1000 R. Honda's 2026 bet on the accessible premium segment.

More expensive to buy and maintain, they target the experienced rider seeking strong sensation and pronounced character. The V-twin is rawer, the V4 is more civilized at high revs.

## Total annual cost of a naked motorcycle

The **purchase** represents only 50-60% of total 5-year cost. To purchase, add insurance, maintenance, gear and fuel.

**Insurance** on a mid-displacement naked (650-900cc) runs **$700-$1,200/year** for an experienced rider with full coverage. On 1000+cc premium bikes (S 1000 R, Streetfighter V4), plan **$1,000-$1,800/year** depending on profile and history.

**Maintenance** annually requires oil change every 5,000-6,000 miles ($90-$170), tires every 5,000-7,500 miles depending on riding style ($350-$520/pair), chain and pads. Plan **$800-$1,500/year** for 5,000 miles annual on mid-displacement, **$1,500-$2,500/year** on premium displacement.

**Fuel** on a naked runs 35-45 mpg, so for 5,000 miles annually about **$700-$1,000/year** in gasoline.

**Rider gear** (helmet, jacket, pants, gloves, boots) lasts 5-7 years if quality. Initial budget **$900-$1,800**, progressive renewal after.

**Typical annual total** over 5 years, excluding purchase: **$2,400-$5,000/year** depending on displacement and mileage.

## FAQ best naked motorcycle

### Which is the best naked motorcycle in 2026?

The **Yamaha MT-07** ($8,499) stays the best-selling naked thanks to the price-performance-reliability balance. For a restricted or beginner rider, it is the default choice. The **Yamaha MT-09** ($11,099) is the polyvalent triple reference. For the sport pinnacle, the **KTM 1390 Super Duke R Evo** ($22,599) or **Ducati Streetfighter V4 S** ($24,995) dominate.

### Twin, triple or inline-4: which to choose?

The **parallel twin** suits daily and restricted licences (low torque, contained budget). The **triple** is the most polyvalent compromise (torque + high revs + sound). The **inline-4** targets the experienced rider who enjoys high revs and smoothness. The **V-twin/V4** brings explosive character for the sport rider. Riding feel varies radically between these 4 characters.

### Restricted or full-licence naked: what price difference?

A restricted-friendly naked (MT-07, Z650, Hornet 750) costs **$7,500-$9,000** new, identical to the unrestricted version. The restriction is a kit installed at the dealer ($300-$600) or via reflashing, removed 2 years after obtaining full licence in many states. On Hornet and MT-09, some models exist directly in unrestricted version. See also options detail in [best A2 motorcycle](/en/blog/best-a2-motorcycle/).

### Which naked motorcycle for daily riding?

For **daily riding** (commute + weekends), target a parallel twin or triple 650-900cc: **Yamaha MT-07**, **Kawasaki Z650**, **Honda CB750 Hornet**, or **Yamaha MT-09**. The 1000+cc are oversized for daily and cost more in insurance and maintenance without practical benefit.

## Which best naked motorcycle to choose in 2026?

The **classification by engine character** stays the most relevant filter. Three principles guide the right choice.

1. **Engine character matters more than horsepower**. A rider who loves vibrations and low torque will be happier on an MT-07 (73 hp) than on an S 1000 R (170 hp) too smooth for their taste. The reverse is true for an inline-4 enthusiast.
2. **Electronics changes everything in 2026**. All 100+ hp nakeds now have lean-sensitive ABS, traction control, ride modes. This makes powerful bikes more usable than 5 years ago, but does not replace experience.
3. **Recent used** (1-3 years) stays the best price-performance ratio. The first 2 years of depreciation (15-25%) benefit secondary buyers who get a still-performant naked.
