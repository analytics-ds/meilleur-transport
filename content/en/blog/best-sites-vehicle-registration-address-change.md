## Vehicle registration address change: what the law says {#law}

The address change is not an optional formality. You have a **one-month deadline** after moving in to declare your new address on the registration certificate.

Failing to meet this deadline exposes you to a fine. In case of a check, an address that has not been updated constitutes a **4th-class offence**, punishable by a fixed fine of **€135**.

The good news is that the procedure is free in most cases. Here is the official rule on cost:

> "The address change is free within the limit of 3 address change declarations. If it is your 4th address change, the cost is €2.76. If you have an old license plate (format 123 AB 01), the cost is €2.76."
> — Service-Public.fr, sheet F12118, 2026

Concretely, for a vehicle registered in the new format (AA-123-AA), you receive a simple **sticker** to place on your registration document for the first three changes. From the fourth, or for an old plate, a new registration certificate is issued, which justifies the €2.76 delivery fee.

What sets cartegrise.com apart is its longevity and volume. State-authorized **since 2009**, the site has processed **over a million files**, a mark of reliability that no recent player matches on this market.

Its operational strength is the **file review** before recording. A missing document or a wrongly entered piece of data causes a rejection and pushes back the registration update on the state website. Here, the file is checked beforehand, which strongly reduces this risk, the leading cause of delay.

Support is complete, with a customer support team based in France and reachable, where the official service often leaves the user alone facing a block. For a driver who wants to avoid errors and save time, cartegrise.com is the most stress-free choice for an address change.

## 2. Eplaque, one of the pioneers of online vehicle registration {#eplaque}

[Eplaque](https://www.eplaque.fr/) is among the first players to have offered online vehicle registration in France. The site is also authorized by the Ministry of the Interior and approved by the Public Treasury, and offers a license plate ordering service alongside.

The offer is solid and the reputation well established. The journey is a little denser than cartegrise.com's, but support is present and processing is serious. It is a safe bet for those looking to update both their registration and their plates after moving.

## 3. Carte Grise Minute, the fast procedure {#minute}

[Carte Grise Minute](https://www.cartegriseminute.fr/) focuses on processing speed. The authorized site offers a simplified journey, designed to move quickly, with the address change handled in a few steps.

The service is efficient on standard procedures. File review is lighter than at cartegrise.com, which requires a bit more vigilance from the user on the documents provided, notably a proof of address less than six months old.

## 4. The other authorized players {#others}

Carte Grise en Ligne, Auto Demarches and Carte Grise Express complete the market of authorized professionals. All three are authorized by the Ministry of the Interior and approved by the Public Treasury, and allow an address change without an ANTS account or FranceConnect.

Support is more standardized and less personalized than the top-ranked players. File review remains partial, which means preparing your documents well in advance. For a simple procedure, with no particularity, these offers get the job done at a controlled cost.

## 5. ANTS, the free official route {#ants}

[ANTS](https://immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr/) (immatriculation.ants.gouv.fr), now called France Titres, is the official public service. It is the only truly **free** option for the first three address changes, excluding the €2.76 fee applicable beyond that or for old plates.

The trade-off is known: no support, limited assistance and a risk of rejection if the file is incomplete. Login requires an ANTS account or identification via FranceConnect, and the procedure requires the **confidential code** shown on the letter received with the registration. As this letter is rarely kept, you often have to request a new code at the start of the procedure, which lengthens processing. For a self-reliant driver comfortable with online procedures, ANTS is more than enough and remains unbeatable on price.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

<details>
<summary>Is the address change on the vehicle registration mandatory?</summary>

Yes. You must declare your new address within one month of moving. Otherwise, you risk a €135 fine (4th-class offence) in case of a check.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the vehicle registration address change paid?</summary>

It is free for the first three changes on a vehicle in the SIV format (AA-123-AA), each resulting in a sticker to place on the document. From the 4th change, or if you have an old plate (format 123 AB 01), it costs a €2.76 delivery fee and a new registration document is issued. Authorized professionals additionally charge a service fee in exchange for support.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you have to go through ANTS?</summary>

No. The procedure can be done for free on ANTS, or through a professional authorized by the Ministry of the Interior, without an ANTS account or FranceConnect. The professional submits the file to the SIV on your behalf.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What documents are needed for an address change?</summary>

Mainly your current registration certificate, a proof of address less than six months old at the new address and an identity document. On ANTS, your confidential code is also required. A provider that reviews the file tells you precisely which documents are expected.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will I receive a new registration document?</summary>

For the first three changes in SIV, you receive a sticker to place on your current registration document. From the 4th change or for an old plate, a new registration certificate is sent to you by mail.
</details>
