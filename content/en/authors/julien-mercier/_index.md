description: "Julien Mercier, mobility and transport expert, tests and compares vehicles and transport solutions for Meilleur Transport."
---

Julien Mercier has been following the mobility and transport sector for more than ten years. He has covered the rise of electric and hybrid vehicles, the evolution of the used-car market and changing usage patterns, from two-wheelers to family cars and freight transport.

On Meilleur Transport, he designs and updates the vehicle comparisons and rankings. His method rests on three principles: cross-checking official manufacturer technical data, comparing available road tests and real-world feedback, and ranking against explicit criteria (budget, real-world use, cost of ownership, reliability). The rankings are independent: no manufacturer or brand pays to be listed or to rank higher.

His goal is simple: help every reader choose the right vehicle for their specific need, without unnecessary jargon and without commercial bias.
